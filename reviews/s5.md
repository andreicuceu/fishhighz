# S5 — Research-ready Python API documentation and validation

S5 documents the user-selected S2–S4 accuracy prescription and provides a
standalone, executable example of its public API conventions. The numerical
prescription, profile identities and retained scientific results are unchanged.
The implementation uses Sol (medium); independent scientific, software and
documentation review uses Sol (high), with coordinator checks.

## Delivered work

- **S5.1:** [RESEARCH_BASELINE.md](../RESEARCH_BASELINE.md) specifies the
  early-weight recurrence, angular/velocity reference and conversions, response
  and noise, mixed-pair damping, full wiggle AP derivatives, fixed fiducial
  covariance, exact two-AP/no-prior fit, bin-1 selection and finite numerical
  qualification. It records the final S2/S3 numerical controls separately from
  public defaults.
- **S5.2:** [README.md](../README.md) and affected public docstrings now describe
  the implemented methods, preparation and optional dependencies. Three
  occurrences of validation errors now list all supported weighting methods.
  [research_bao_forecast.py](../examples/research_bao_forecast.py) uses generated
  inputs, explicit early weights and the full covariance of forest auto,
  forest–galaxy cross and galaxy auto, with only `ap` and `at` varied and no
  priors. The source distribution includes the guide and examples.
- **S5.3:** Focused tests, source-distribution and installed-wheel checks verify
  that the example is usable without neighboring checkouts. Existing S2–S4
  evidence supplies the scientific qualification of the unchanged prescription.

The example's synthetic joint errors are `(0.0468765060, 0.0225581179)` for
`(ap, at)`. Its early weights converge after 10 updates with doubled-count
confirmation. Its joint Fisher differs from the sum of the three individual
Fishers, as required when their covariance is retained. These illustrative
numbers are not a DESI-2 prediction or a new convergence study.

## Scientific limits and disposition

S5 changes no BAO constraint. The accepted baseline still uses the legacy
angular/velocity reference mode, the tested mixed-pair damping assumption and
full wiggle AP derivatives. Numerical qualification is finite and case-specific;
it does not establish a continuum bound, physically optimal weighting or an
independently validated reconstruction prescription.

D1 reference-mode sensitivity and optional D2 early-versus-W12 comparison remain
in [DEFERRED_SCIENTIFIC_TESTS.md](../DEFERRED_SCIENTIFIC_TESTS.md). CLI/configuration
and general serialization remain outside this Python-API milestone. No new
real-survey forecasts, historical reruns, Slurm actions, commits or pushes were
performed. Historical Step 13, W12 and W-series assignments remain closed.

Independent scientific, software and documentation review **passes**, with no
outstanding corrections. Its direct Wick contraction reproduces the joint Fisher
to a relative residual of `4.50e-17`; 14 focused independent checks pass. Final
coordinator verification also passes. S5 is complete and ready for user review;
no subsequent step has been started.
