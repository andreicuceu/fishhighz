## 1. Setup (Sec. 2.1)
- Window, Eq. (1): W(x) = nbar^-1 sum_n delta_D(x_perp - x_perp,n), nbar = N/A (A = survey area on sky, comoving Mpc^2); line of sight treated as continuous. Measured field delta = delta_F W, delta_F = F/<F> - 1.
- Eq. (2): covariance of delta-modes contains sum_{n,m} w~_n(k_par) w~_m^* e^{-i(k_perp.x_n - k'_perp.x_m)} [P_N,n delta^m_n + int d^2k''_perp/(2pi)^2 e^{ik''.(x_n-x_m)} P_F(k_par,k''_perp)]. Weight normalisation: N^-1 sum_n w~_n(k_par) = 1. Assumes the joint weight of sightlines n,m factorises (w_n w_m); noise uncorrelated between sightlines; transverse modes continuous (A -> infinity).
- Eqs. (3),(4): P_N,n = <F>^-2 [S/N]_DeltaX^-2 (DeltaX/1 Mpc) = 0.8 <F>^-2 [S/N]_Dlambda^-2 (Dlambda/1 A)(1+z)/4)^{-3/2}; white noise assumed. Units: Mpc.

## 2. Power estimator and variance (Sec. 2.1)
- Eq. (5): P_tot = <|delta~_k|^2> = P_F(k) + nbar^-1 [ P_N-bar + (w~^2)-bar P_los(k_par) ].
- Eq. (6): P_los(k_par) = int d^2k_perp/(2pi)^2 P_F(k_par,k_perp) (1D power; the "aliasing" term, name taken from McDonald & Eisenstein 2007).
- Eq. (7): P_N-bar = N^-1 sum_n |w~_n|^2 P_N,n.
- The paper states Eq. (5) agrees with Eq. (13) of ME07 and Eq. (A6) of White et al. 2010. Derivation: sum over skewers = Monte-Carlo integral of plane wave; m != n terms give two delta functions (P_F), m = n gives one (P_tot - P_F).
- Estimator: P^_F = |delta~_k|^2 with pixel correlations along a single sightline (m = n terms) omitted (Sec. 2.1, before Eq. 8). Eq. (8): <P^_F> = P_F. Biased at fractional order N^-1/2; the bias term is removable since x_perp,n are known (Sec. 3, subtraction of tr[Q^(0) C]).
- Eq. (9): general covariance (Gaussian; fourth cumulant dropped; "decent approximation at >10 Mpc modes").
- Eq. (10): cov[P^_F(k),P^_F(k')]_{k_par} = 2 P_tot^2 delta_{kk'} + (4/N) (w~^2)-bar P_F(k) P_F(k') + two "beat-coupling" convolution terms with (1/N)(w~^2)-bar^2 nbar^-1 int d^2k^(1)/(2pi)^2 P_F P_F. Off-diagonal terms neglected; they do not average down with mode number (beat coupling); (4/N)P_F P_F' matches diagonal when number of binned modes ~ N, i.e. for k_perp > 0.06 [nbar/1e-3 Mpc^-2]^{1/2} (k_perp/Delta k_perp)^{1/2} Mpc^-1. They always sit below the aliasing term nbar^-1 P_F in P_tot for relevant nbar, and they "cap" total information (addresses ME07 issue). Diagonal variance used henceforth: var[P^_F] = 2 P_tot^2 (no explicit per-mode counting factor: per-mode variance of a complex-mode |delta|^2).
- Footnote 3: sum P_F/P_tot over wavevectors at fixed k_par <= N (information <= number of pixels).
- Clustering of sources: term nbar^-1(P_N + P_los) multiplied by 1 + C_q(k_perp) nbar (C_q = angular power spectrum of sources); relevant only for nbar >~ 1e-2 Mpc^-2.

## 3. Optimal weights (Sec. 2.2)
- Eq. (11): w~_n(k_par) = B(k_par) [P_los(k_par) + P_N,n]^-1. B fixed by normalisation, independent of n. Derived by maximising P_F^2/var[P^_F] with var = 2 P_tot^2, i.e. minimising the n-dependent piece nbar^-1 mean(w^2 (P_los + P_N,n)) subject to mean(w) = 1 (my reading; the paper only states the result).
- Dependence: on k_par only through P_los(k_par) and P_N,n(k_par) (constant if noise white and P_los white); NO dependence on k_perp, on P_F(3D), or on nbar. P_1D (P_los) enters as an extra per-sightline variance added to the noise, on the same footing as P_N,n (i.e. aliasing is treated as noise). Weight is a per-sightline (not per-pixel) function.
- Eq. (12): with these weights P_tot = P_F(k) + nbar_eff^-1 P_los(k_par).
- Eq. (13): nbar_eff = A^-1 sum_n nu_n, nu_n = P_los/(P_los + P_N,n) (0 to 1). Consistency check (mine): with B = [mean(1/(P_los+P_N,n))]^-1, nbar^-1 mean(w^2 (P_los+P_N,n)) = nbar^-1 P_los N/sum(nu_n) = nbar_eff^-1 P_los.
- Relation to weight moments: with sum w = N, nbar_eff^-1 P_los = nbar^-1 [mean(w^2 P_N,n) + mean(w^2) P_los] (Eqs. 5,7); nbar_eff = nbar * mean(nu) (Eq. 13) only after optimal w. Not given by the paper in terms of sum w, sum w^2 sigma^2 explicitly.
- Weak k_par dependence of nbar_eff: P_los ~ white for k <= 0.5 Mpc^-1 (Table 2, McDonald et al. 2005 data); factor 2 smaller P_los gives factor 1.4 lower nbar_eff for BOSS-like z = 2.5. Table 3 lists nu_n vs [S/N]_1A (P_N < P_los at S/N_1A ~ 2).
- Gain over uniform weighting: for power-law LF slope -2, S/N ~ flux, P_N,lim/P_los = 1: 4% in (P_tot - P_F); for 0.5 (0.3): 40% (60%). Weighting matters only when a large fraction of sightlines have P_N,n/P_los < 1.
- Comparison with FKP: paper's analogy is Eq. (15) V_eff,g = V_g [P_g/(P_g + nbar_g,3D^-1)]^2 -> Eq. (16) V_eff(k) = A L [P_F/(P_F + nbar_eff^-1 P_los(k_par))]^2. Difference: shot term is in the sky plane, modulated by P_los. The paper does NOT write an FKP weight w = P/(P+P_N) for the Lya field; Eq. (11) is not FKP-like in the sense of containing P_F: with the aliasing term in P_los, weights are w ~ 1/(P_los + P_N,n) (aliasing in the denominator, together with noise), while a naive noise-only weight would be 1/P_N,n and a naive FKP would need P_F + ... (which does not appear because P_F is common to all sightlines). Amplitude-form: nu_n = P_los/(P_los+P_N,n) = 1/(1+P_N,n/P_los) is FKP-like with P_los playing "signal", P_N,n "noise" (my interpretation).
- Simplified weight (Sec. 2.4, Sec. 6): weight each sightline by (1 + sigma_N^2/sigma_los^2)^-1, sigma^2 = noise and Lya-forest variance smoothed on >= 10 Mpc so that P_los is white; weak dependence on smoothing scale; w~_n(0) alone is sufficient for xi (identical if P_los, P_N white; k_par <~ 0.5 Mpc^-1 => w~(k_par) ~ w~(0)). Applicable with sky lines / DLA holes.

## 4. Effective volume and S/N (Sec. 2.3)
- Eq. (17): delta P_F,mu = ( int_0^1 dmu (1+g mu^2)^4 var[P^_F(k,mu)]^-1 )^{-1/2}, P_F,mu = P_F/(1+g mu^2)^2; V_eff,mu = 2 A L P_F,mu^2/delta P_F,mu^2.
- Mode counting: N_k = k^2 Delta k A L/(2 pi^2) = 4 pi k^2 dk V/(2pi)^3 independent samples in a shell, precision delta P_F,mu/N_k^{1/2}. Eq. (18): S/N = 60 (V_eff,mu/AL /1e-2)^{1/2} (L/1 Gpc * A/10^3 deg^2)^{1/2} ((1+z)/4)^{1.2} (k^3 [Delta k/k]/1e-3 Mpc^-3)^{1/2}.
- AMBIGUITY: Eq. (36) uses (1/2) sum over "all independent modes" with P_tot^-2 dP/dlambda dP/dlambda; whether half-space or full-space sum is intended is not stated; V_eff definitions with factor 2 in Eq. (17) suggest a full-k count with var = 2 P_tot^2 (V_eff = A L when P_tot = P_F).
- Fig. 3: nbar_eff ~ 1e-3 Mpc^-2 is critical density for sample-variance limitation at k <~ 0.1 Mpc^-1.

## 5. Quadratic estimator (Sec. 3) and iteration
- Eqs. (19)-(22): <delta~_F,n delta~_F,m> = C_nm + P_F(k) X_nm; C_nm = P(k_par,r_nm) + P_N,n delta_nm; X_nm = (Delta k_perp/2pi)^2 exp[i k_perp.r_nm]; P(k_par,r) ~ int dk_perp/(2pi) k_perp P_F J_0(k_perp r) (assumes P_F X << P).
- Eq. (23): P^QE = [P^QE]_last + sum Q_nm delta~_n delta~_m - tr[QC] (iterated). Eq. (24): Q = (tr[C^-1 X C^-1 X])^-1 C^-1 X C^-1. Eq. (25): var = 2 tr[C Q C Q] (Gaussian; excludes sample variance 2 P_F^2); unbiased: tr[Q X] = 1.
- Zeroth order (off-diagonal C_nm -> 0, justified as sightline separations >~ 10 Mpc where P(k_par,r_perp) is down by >~ 4): Eq. (26) Q^(0)_nm = A (2pi/Delta k_perp)^2 e^{ik.r_nm}/[(P_los+P_N,n)(P_los+P_N,m)], Eq. (27) A = (sum_nm ...)^-1, Eq. (28) A ~ (sum_n (P_los+P_N,n)^-1)^-2 (drops m = n terms). Same weight as Eq. (11): "identical", stated. Estimator remains unbiased after subtracting tr[Q^(0) C] (corrects the N^-1/2 bias). Correlation-function estimator Eq. (29): xi^(0)(r) = sum_{nm at fixed r} [w_n * delta_F(x_n)] * [w_m * delta_F(x_m)], * = line-of-sight convolution.
- Efficiency: variance of P^(0) exceeds P^QE by only 6% (nbar = 1e-3), 10% (3e-3), 20% (1e-2 Mpc^-2) at smallest k_perp, zero noise, no sample variance, k_par = 0.1 (Fig. 4); noise reduces excess roughly in proportion to nbar_eff reduction (factor 0.7).
- Iteration: Eqs. (30)-(31) Gauss-Seidel [C_GS^(i)]^-1 = L^-1 (I - U [C_GS^(i-1)]^-1), initialised with diagonal C (giving Q^(0)); Jacobi first order [C_J^(1)]^-1 = [C_J^(0)]^-1 (2C_J^(0) - C) [C_J^(0)]^-1. Eq. (32) Q^(1)_J,nm = w~_n w~_m e^{ik.r_nm} (1 - S~_n)(1 - S~_m^*), Eq. (33) S~_i = sum_{j != i} P(k_par,r_ij)/(P_los+P_N,j) exp[-i k_perp.r_ij]; <S_i> = P_F(k)/(nbar_eff^-1 P_los). Down-weights sightlines with excess neighbours within |k_perp.r| <~ 1. "Iterating" here is on C^-1 (and [P^QE]_last), not on weights self-consistently with P_F; the Sec. 2.2 weights use P_los, which is taken as known/measured (self-consistency of P_los input not discussed; sole note: weights need P_los and noise).

## 6. Cross-correlation (Sec. 4.2)
- Eq. (34): var[P^_Fg] = P_Fg^2 + P_tot (P_g + nbar_g,3D^-1). Same weights (Eq. 11) maximise S/N if weight factorises. Eq. (35): cross beats Lya auto when n_g,3D > nbar_eff P_los^-1 b^2/b_g^2.

## 7. Fisher (Sec. 4.4)
- Eq. (36): F_ij = (1/2) sum_k P_tot(k)^-2 dP_F/dlambda_i dP_F/dlambda_j, Gaussian, sum over independent modes; first three line-of-sight modes omitted (continuum/mean-flux contamination); parameters: amplitude, tilt, running, g, D_A, H. Conservative BAO-only variant subtracts no-wiggle P_F before differentiating. Scaling of errors (L A)^-1/2. Results: BOSS-like nbar_eff = 5e-4: sigma(D_A)/D_A ~ 0.02-0.03; BigBOSS nbar_eff = 1.5e-3: 0.010-0.015.
- Model: Eq. (14) P_F = b^2 (1+g mu^2)^2 P_lin exp(-k_par^2/k_D^2), k_D = 0.08 s/km (T = 2e4 K), b calibrated to P_los(0.0014 s/km) of McDonald et al. 2005, g = 1.

## 8. Systematics and continuum (Sec. 5)
- Continuum errors uncorrelated between sightlines add to noise: term nbar^-1 P_los^cont in P_tot (Sec. 5); < 1% of P_F at k_par > 0.1 Mpc^-1. Omitting m = n correlations removes leading continuum/DLA-wing contamination (mostly line-of-sight correlations). Eq. (37) sigma_<F>^2 ~ (P_los + P_N)/(Delta chi N). Mean-flux error affects only purely line-of-sight modes; drop first modes. Damping wings: shot-like, smaller in 3D.

## 9. Explicit approximations (collected)
Gaussian delta_F (Eq. 9); diagonal covariance (off-diagonal/beat-coupling dropped, Eq. 10); large N (Monte-Carlo integral -> delta function, O(N^-1/2) errors); independent, white per-sightline noise (Eqs. 3-4); no in-sightline pixel correlations used (m = n omitted); factorised weights w_n w_m; sightline correlations P(k_par,r_nm) neglected in Q^(0) (r >~ 10 Mpc); clustering of quasars neglected (nbar < 1e-2); P_los assumed ~ white for k_par < 0.5 Mpc^-1; noise homogeneous along a sightline (inhomogeneity handled only heuristically via smoothed variance ratio, Sec. 2.4).

## 10. Ambiguities for review
1. Eq. (5) overbars lost in extraction; identification of (w~^2)-bar P_los and P_N-bar with normalisation mean(w) = 1 is my reconstruction; it is consistent with Eqs. (11)-(13).
2. Whether the Fisher/mode-count uses half of k-space (Eq. 36 "all independent modes" vs N_k after Eq. 17) is unstated.
3. w depends on P_los evaluated at the k_par of the mode; paper argues it is effectively k-independent, so a single scalar weight per sightline is recommended in practice.
4. Paper never gives n_eff in terms of (sum w, sum w^2, sum w^2 sigma^2) beyond the optimal-weight case.
