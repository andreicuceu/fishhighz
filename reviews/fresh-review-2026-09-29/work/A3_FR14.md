## 1. General Fisher framework (Sec. 2.3, "Fisher matrix parameter errors")
- (11) $F_{ij}=\partial_i\bar{\mathbf O}^T\,\mathbf C^{-1}\,\partial_j\bar{\mathbf O}$. Gaussian likelihood, parameter-independent covariance. Experiments are independent and Fisher matrices add.
- (4) sound horizon $s=\int_0^{a_\star} c_s/(a^2H)\,da$. The observables $D_A/s$ and $Hs$ carry the parameter dependence of $s$.

## 2. Galaxy/QSO clustering (Sec. 4.1)
- (15) $P_{ij}(k,\mu)=(b_i+f\mu^2)(b_j+f\mu^2)P_{\rm mass}(k)+\bar n_i^{-1}\delta^K_{ij}$, with $f=d\ln D/d\ln a$ and linear-theory $P_{\rm mass}$.
- Bias models are all of the form $b(z)D(z)=$ const, with $D(0)=1$:

| Tracer | $b(z)D(z)$ |
|---|---|
| ELG (DESI) | 0.84 |
| LRG (BOSS and DESI) | 1.7 |
| QSO (DESI) | 1.2 |
| HETDEX | 0.89 |
| Euclid | 0.76 (also used for WFIRST) |

- The BOSS DR9 comparison finds $bD=1.48$ instead of 1.7, i.e. the fiducial LRG bias is optimistic. This is stated in the BOSS section.
- Number densities are tabulated per $dz\,d{\rm deg}^2$ (Tables for DESI, BOSS, eBOSS, Euclid, WFIRST), together with $\bar nP_{0.2,0}$ and $\bar nP_{0.14,0.6}$.

## 3. BAO Fisher (Sec. 4.1.1, "BAO")
- The paper uses a lightly modified Seo & Eisenstein 2007 code.
- (16) $P_{\rm BAO,nl}(k,\mu)=P_{\rm BAO,lin}(k,\mu)\exp[-k_\perp^2\Sigma_\perp^2/2-k_\parallel^2\Sigma_\parallel^2/2]$. Here $P_{\rm BAO,lin}$ includes bias and RSD.
- $\Sigma_\perp=9.4\,(\sigma_8(z)/0.9)\ h^{-1}$Mpc and $\Sigma_\parallel=(1+f(z))\Sigma_\perp$. Redshift scaling enters through $\sigma_8(z)$ and $f(z)$ only.
- The damping and RSD factors are taken OUTSIDE the derivatives. This avoids using their structure as a ruler. The effect is that the covariance is inflated by the inverse damping factor.
- "50% reconstruction": both $\Sigma$ are multiplied by $r(\bar nP)$, with $r=0.5$ at high density.
  - $r$ is interpolated over $r=(1.0,0.9,0.8,0.70,0.6,0.55,0.52,0.5)$ at $x=(0.2,0.3,0.5,1,2,3,6,10)$.
  - $x\equiv\bar nP(k{=}0.14\,h{\rm Mpc}^{-1},\mu{=}0.6)/0.1734$.
  - $r=1$ for $x<0.2$ and $r=0.5$ for $x>10$. This is a rough degradation following Padmanabhan et al. 2012.
- The covariance used is still the full linear $P$ with shot noise, Eq. 15.
- The derivative is taken of the wiggles-only $P$. For Lyα this is stated as "subtracting a smoothed version of the power spectrum from the wiggly one". Galaxy details are not spelled out beyond "the code that accompanies" SE07 (the cos²-averaging derivative approximation of SE07 is mentioned only in the Lyα paragraph).
- The parameters are $D_A/s$ and $Hs$. The galaxy/QSO correlation coefficient is about 0.4.
- Isotropic dilation: $D_A=(1+\delta\ln R)D_{A,\rm fid}$ and $H=(1+\delta\ln R)^{-1}H_{\rm fid}$ (unnumbered, between Eqs. 16 and 17). It is used only for summary plots.
- Multi-tracer galaxies: $(\bar nP)_{\rm comb}(k,\mu)=\sum_i\bar n_iP_i(k,\mu)$, i.e. optimal mode-by-mode S/N addition.
- The $d\mu/d\alpha$ treatment is not spelled out in the paper. The code's handling is only implied ("direct Fisher derivatives of wiggles-only power", Lyα paragraph). Ambiguous.
- No explicit broadband nuisance marginalisation appears in the isolated-BAO calculation. Only the wiggles-only derivative is used.

## 4. Broadband and covariance (Sec. 4.1.2)
- (19) $P^{\rm obs}_{ij}(k^{\rm obs}_\parallel,k^{\rm obs}_\perp)=aH\,r^{-2}\,P^{\rm com}_{ij}[aHk^{\rm obs}_\parallel,r^{-1}k^{\rm obs}_\perp]$ (observed coordinates: km/s and degrees).
- (20) $\langle\Delta P_{ij}\Delta P_{mn}\rangle=\dfrac{2\pi^2}{Vk^2\Delta k\Delta\mu}(P_{im}P_{jn}+P_{in}P_{jm})$, for $0<\mu<1$.
  - It is valid in the small-bin limit, so in practice an integral.
  - Bins are independent.
  - It is Gaussian.
- Bias: one free parameter per $\Delta z=0.1$ bin per tracer.
- $k_{\max}^{\rm eff}=0.1$ or $0.2\ h{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$ for broadband. Above it, BAO-only information is used, with modes $k<k^{\rm eff}_{\max}$ removed from the BAO integral.
- The same damping factor (Eq. 16, outside the derivatives) and the same reconstruction factor are applied to the broadband signal.

## 5. Lyα forest (Sec. 4.2 "LyaF", Eqs. 21-29)
- (21) $P_F=b_F^2(1+\beta_F\mu^2)^2P_{\rm mass}(k,z)D(k,\mu,z)$. This is the McDonald & Eisenstein 2003 formula.
- (22) $D=\exp\{(k/k_{NL})^{\alpha_{NL}}-(k/k_P)^{\alpha_P}-[k_\parallel/k_V(k)]^{\alpha_V}\}$, with $k_V=k_{V0}(1+k/k_V')^{\alpha_V'}$.
  - $k_{NL}=6.4$, $\alpha_{NL}=0.569$.
  - $k_P\sim15.3$, $\alpha_P\sim2.01$.
  - $k_{V0}\sim1.22$, $\alpha_V\sim1.5$, $k_V'\sim0.923\ h{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$, $\alpha_V'\sim0.451$.
- For BAO the central MEO03 model is used at all $z$, multiplied by $((1+z)/3.2)^{3.8}$ to match the 1D power evolution. The values of $b_F$ and $\beta_F$ are NOT quoted; they are "from Table I of MEO03". Ambiguous.
- (23) $P_F^{\rm 3D,obs}(\mathbf k)=P_F^{\rm 3D}(\mathbf k)+P_F^{\rm 1D}(k_\parallel)P_w^{\rm 2D}+P_N^{\rm eff}$ (McDonald & Eisenstein 2007, hypothetical 3D FT).
- (24) $P_w^{\rm 2D}=I_2/(I_1^2L_q)$.
- (25) $P_N^{\rm eff}=I_3l_p/(I_1^2L_q)$. Here $L_q$ is the forest length and $l_p$ the pixel width.
- (26)-(28) $I_1=\int dm\,\frac{dn_q}{dm}w$, $I_2=\int dm\,\frac{dn_q}{dm}w^2$, $I_3=\int dm\,\frac{dn_q}{dm}\sigma_N^2(m)w^2$.
- (29) $w(m)=\dfrac{P_S/P_N(m)}{1+P_S/P_N(m)}$, with $P_N(m)=\sigma_N^2(m)l_p/(I_1L_q)$.
  - This is iterative, since $I_1$ depends on $w$.
  - $P_S$ is evaluated at $k=0.07\ h{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$, $\mu=0.5$ (after Feldman-Kaiser-Peacock 1994).
- $k_\perp$ is restricted to below the Nyquist frequency set by the mean quasar separation.
- Forest range: $985<\lambda_{\rm rf}<1200$ Å for BAO (Lyβ included), and $1041<\lambda<1185$ Å for broadband.
- The noise term is divided by $(1+\beta\mu^2)^2$ rather than including that factor in the derivative, which avoids artificial breaking of the $H$-$D_A$ degeneracy. This departs from McDonald & Eisenstein 2007.
- The damping factors of SE07 are used with NO reconstruction.
- Agreement with SE07 is quoted at percent level for matching data sets.
- DESI inputs: quasar LF from Palanque-Delabrouille 2013 for $g<23$, times 0.8 for targeting inefficiency. Spectral S/N per Å comes from BBspecsim (a figure). Only the blue arm is used.
- BOSS inputs: Jiang 2006 LF for $g<22$, times 0.73.
- Not given in the paper: $l_p$ and $L_q$ in pixels or Mpc, the resolution or pixel-smoothing (FWHM vs $\sigma$, definition of $R$), the explicit $P_{1D}$ formula, and $\sigma_N(m)$ other than via a figure. No resolution or window damping is written down. Ambiguous or absent.
- Lyα broadband and 1D power:
  - Parameter dependence is from MEO03 Table I. The 1D power from ~100 high-resolution spectra is added.
  - The broadband analysis uses $z=2$-$2.7$ only, and does NOT use the QSO cross-correlation.

## 6. Lyα x QSO cross-correlation (Sec. 4.3)
- The multi-tracer model is $o_i=c_i\delta_m+\epsilon_i$, with $\langle|\epsilon_i|^2\rangle=N_i$ uncorrelated. The combined noise is $(\sum_ic_i^2N_i^{-1})^{-1}$.
- (unnumbered) $\sigma_{\rm BAO}=\sigma_{{\rm BAO},V}[1+(\sum_iP_iN_i^{-1})^{-1}]$, evaluated at the BAO-typical $(k,\mu)$.
- (unnumbered) $\sigma_{{\rm BAO},i}=\sigma_{{\rm BAO},V}[1+N_iP_i^{-1}]$.
- High-noise limit: $\sigma_{\rm BAO}=(\sum_i\sigma_{{\rm BAO},i}^{-1})^{-1}$. This is the inverse SUM, not the quadrature sum.
- Ingredients in this approximation:
  - The Lyα and QSO BAO errors are computed separately and then combined.
  - There is no cross-noise term.
  - Reconstruction is not applied to Lyα.
  - The QSO auto-power damping/reconstruction follows Sec. 3.
- Check at $z\sim2.5$: $\bar nP(0.14,0.6)=0.09$ for the DESI QSO and ~0.22 for Lyα (scaled from its BAO error). The paper calls this "borderline" for the high-noise approximation.
- It states a possible 10-15% gain from reconstruction, which is not included.

## 7. Explicit approximations (list)
1. Gaussian likelihood and parameter-independent covariance; experiments independent.
2. Fine $k$, $\mu$ bins, independent, with Eq. 20 in the small-bin limit.
3. Linear theory, constant $bD$, and a $\Sigma$ damping that is not tuned per tracer.
4. Reconstruction as a scalar $r(\bar nP)$ applied to both $\Sigma$.
5. Damping and RSD factors pulled outside the derivatives.
6. The Lyα $P_S$ weights at a single $(k,\mu)=(0.07,0.5)$, with the iterative $w(m)$.
7. Nyquist cut on $k_\perp$.
8. The Lyα and QSO BAO combination done in the high-noise limit, not a full multi-tracer Fisher.
9. The Lyα model taken from MEO03 at its central $z\simeq2.25$ parameters, times $((1+z)/3.2)^{3.8}$.
10. No Lyα cross-noise correlation with QSOs, and no non-Gaussian, bispectrum or systematics terms.

Ambiguity note for Sec. 6: a full Lyα x QSO multi-tracer Fisher is said to be possible (as done for ELG/LRG/QSO) but is not performed.
