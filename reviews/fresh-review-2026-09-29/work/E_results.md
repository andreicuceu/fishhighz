# E_results: numerical results of the fishhighz (fh) vs lyaforecast (lf) DESI-2 15×2pt forecast set

Inputs: 107 completed runs (79 fh, 26 lf, 2 fh compatibility profiles) out of 108 launched; `fh_t_w_mcdonald` failed by design (McDonald weights capped at 96 updates for lya(lbg), bin 2), `fh_r3_mcd2000` is the 2000-update retry. Harness `/pscratch/sd/a/acuceu/fishhighz-review/harness/`; analysis code `analyse2.py`, `analyse2_checks.py`, `analyse2_extra.py`. Machine-readable table: `appendix/runs_summary.csv` (every σ: combined, 6 per-bin joints, 5 key spectra per bin and combined). Figures: `appendix/figures/fig1..fig5*.png`.

Conventions. σ(α∥), σ(α⊥) from the 2×2 Fisher blocks. "Combined" = sum of the six per-bin 2×2 Fisher matrices (common α∥, α⊥), identical for both codes. "Joint" = fh's own selection (bin 1: lya(qso)², lya(qso)×qso, qso²; bins 2–6: all 15 spectra); the lf joint is `joint_fhsel` (same bin-1 selection), so all fh–lf comparisons are like-for-like. Δ = σ/σ_baseline − 1 in percent, against the baseline of the same code (`fh_baseline` for all fh cases, `lf_baseline` for lf cases) unless stated. Key spectra: lya(qso) auto, lya(lbg) auto, qso auto, lya(qso)×qso, lbg auto. fh runs with `individuals=key` carry the first four only, so "lbg auto" exists for fh only in `fh_baseline`, `fh_full_compat`, `fh_fixed_compat`, `fh_cum6_lf`, `fh_r1_invvar`. fh bin 1 contains no lya(lbg) or lbg auto; the combined lya(lbg)/lbg autos of fh therefore cover bins 2–6 and those of lf bins 1–6 (the lf bin-1 lya(lbg) auto, σ ≈ 1.5–2.1, contributes nothing). Per-bin key-spectrum σ are tabulated for the baselines, the compat profiles and the chain endpoint; for every other case the per-bin key-spectrum values are in the CSV, and the tables give per-bin joint Δ and combined key-spectrum Δ.

## Headline results

* fh_baseline vs lf_baseline (joint, combined): σ∥ = 0.007191 vs 0.007625 (fh/lf = 0.942), σ⊥ = 0.005020 vs 0.005205 (0.965). Per bin fh/lf−1 runs from −2.2% (bin 1) to −10.3% (bin 6) in ∥ and from +0.35% (bin 1) to −7.2% (bin 6) in ⊥.
* Live-capture `fh_full_compat` reproduces `lf_baseline` to 1.3e-15 in every per-bin joint and key spectrum (70 comparisons): the fh Wick/Fisher machinery is identical to lf once fed lf's arrays and prefix-3-iteration weights.
* The convention chain (legacy-3 weights, c/R, lf damping, EdS, arithmetic z, lf-like AP derivative) brings the fh joint to +0.35% (∥) and +0.55% (⊥) of lf: it closes to better than 0.6% (per bin ≤ +0.7%). The dominant steps are legacy-3 weights (+3.1/+3.0%) and the lf cross-damping rule (+3.1/+1.5%).
* Largest sensitivities of the joint (combined) to inputs that are not lf-vs-fh conventions: galaxy/forest density ×0.5 (+36/+49%), reconstruction off (+10.4/+6.4%), max magnitude 23.75 (+10/+19%), forest bias ±15% (∓4%), σ_z = 4 Mpc/h (+9.1/+2.7%), literal-bias runs (see Anomalies).
* Inverse-variance forest weights (C1 R1): joint −1.83/−1.85%; forest autos −6.8/−9.2% (lya(qso)) and −7.4/−10.0% (lya(lbg)).

5. **Toggles that move σ in the counter-intuitive direction:** legacy-3 improves QSO-forest autos at z<2.7 (−3%) and bin-1 joint (−1.5%) while worsening high z (+5.8%) and lya(lbg) (+42%); `fh_t_ap_q1` leaves σ∥ unchanged and lowers σ⊥ (joint −1.17%); more galaxy shot-noise depth via clamped LBG/LAE in bin 1 improves the joint (−1.55%).
6. **`fh_qso_b12` and `fh_lae_b089` are literal-b readings.** With b_QSO = 1.2 against the fiducial ≈3.5–3.8 the qso auto σ rises by ~3–5× (+287/+487%). If FR14's 1.2 is bD (b × D(z)), then at z ≈ 2.4 the fiducial b·D is ≈ 1.2 (D ≈ 0.3, my approximate estimate, not computed here), so the intended run is probably ≈ the baseline; these two runs are not valid brackets of the reviewers' item and should be redefined in b·D (growth-normalised) terms.
7. **Fixed-reference weights with unconverged McDonald:** McDonald needs 624 updates for lya(lbg) bin 2 (cap 96); the ME07 amplitude recurrence is slow there. All other 106 completed cases have max 54 updates.
8. **Structural nulls:** `fh_r6_nyq` (<0.01%), `fh_znorm2p2` (identical to baseline), `fh_r2_invvar_k01` ≈ R1 (3e-5). `fh_dzero_*` and `fh_bin1_*` change nothing in the bins they do not touch (exactly 0).
9. `fh_full_compat` records no per-forest weight status (compat wrapper); weights there are the 3-iteration prefix scheme by construction.
