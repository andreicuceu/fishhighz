# F1: adversarial verification of the fishhighz vs lyaforecast review claims

## Scope and method

Each claim was tested against four sources: the package code (fishhighz at HEAD 456129b with an unmodified working tree; lyaforecast read-only), the arXiv LaTeX sources (ME07 astro-ph/0607122, MW11 1102.1752, FR14 1308.4164, plus eBOSS DR16 2007.08995 and DESI DR1 Lyα BAO 2404.03001 for F6), the saved `runs/<case>/result.json`, and the harness code (`toggles2.py`, `TOGGLES.md`). Everything was downloaded and computed in `scratchpad/F1/`.

Independent checks:
- `wcheck.py` is my own implementation of the early, prefix, inverse-variance and minimum-variance weights. It runs on the production forest inputs saved by C1 (`scratchpad/C1/inputs.pkl`) and reproduces the baseline A and P_pix of all 11 forest×bin entries to every printed digit.
- A McDonald and FR14-literal recurrence on LBG bin 2.
- B(k∥)/B(ref) from `fishhighz.models.p1d.default_p1d` × response.
- A parse test of per-category `k_max` in BAO and bao_marginalized INIs.
- A test of scipy `RectBivariateSpline` clamping outside its bbox.
- Recomputation of the combined σ of 9 cases from the saved Fisher matrices. This reproduces E to all quoted digits.
- The single failing pytest.

No forecast was run and no package or run file was modified.

**Information barrier.** No work file cites `docs/archive/` or other review trees. The two apparent leaks are both legitimate:
- The only archived number used, the McDonald bin-2 amplitude 0.8668 at update 96 (C1), is quoted in `docs/research/FOREST_WEIGHTING_DECISION.md:318`, which is allowed.
- The "S4 attribution" (C2) is referenced through RESEARCH_BASELINE.

The barrier holds.

## Inconsistencies between reviewers, resolved

1. **D1 F1 vs C1 (early-weight excess).** D1 gives 0.1–1%; C1 gives 2–22%. My reproduction on production inputs gives N_eff/N_min = 1.002–1.216, and E R1 gives −1.8% joint. C1 is right. D1's toy used unrepresentative S·L·I1/B.
2. **B row 23 vs C2 §8 (μ grid).** B says lf is "σ⊥ 2% optimistic"; C2 says it is pessimistic in σ∥. E `lf_nmu20`/`lf_nmu5` support C2.
3. **B row 26 vs C2 §1 (AP toy).** B gives 1.009/1.015; C2 gives 0.999/0.995. E `fh_t_ap_lf` supports C2.
4. **B row 9 and C2 §3 vs E (resolution).** The estimates of ~1% compare with the forecast's +0.23%. E is decisive.
5. **B row 8 vs C2/E (mixed damping).** B estimated 1–2% on the joint; the forecast gives +3.3/+1.65%.
6. **C2 §8 vs E (lf k grid).** C2 calls the lf k grid "irrelevant"; E finds lf's k dependence at 0.9%. Not contradictory: C2 used fh's analytic derivative on the lf grid, and the lf sensitivity enters through lf's backward FD dk/k scaling. C2 §1 itself finds lf-exact 0.1–0.9% optimistic.
7. **C1 vs E (McDonald update count).** C1 gives ≈300 updates; E gives 624. The solver returns the doubled-count state 2t, and I measure 312 → 624.
8. **B row 17 vs production (McDonald bin label).** B has McDonald unconverged in lya(lbg) "bin 1". Production excludes lya(lbg) from bin 1, and the slow case is bin 2 (E, C1, decision record §5).
