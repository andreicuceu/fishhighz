# Active research

The adopted S2–S4 research prescription uses early-lyaforecast forest weighting,
physical FWHM resolution, per-field damping, full wiggle AP derivatives, and
fixed fiducial weights and covariance. The baseline document describes the
precise prescription and the limits of its qualification.

The weighting decision retains McDonald weighting as an explicit alternative.
It does not imply that every alternative is available through the native INI.
The deferred-tests record distinguishes agreed studies from proposals; neither
an archived instruction nor a passing numerical review authorizes a new study.
Finite-refinement checks and iteration convergence do not establish a continuum
limit or physical accuracy of reconstruction assumptions.

These records retain their original scientific decisions and dated evidence.
The user guide describes the current interface; the archives preserve earlier
implementations, including failed or scientifically unaccepted forecasts.
