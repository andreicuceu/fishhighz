## Coverage, plots and qualification

For each profile, the table contains 78 available individual results, six available joint results and 12 explicit bin-1 selection exclusions. All 84 available matrices have rank two; there are no failed or unconstrained selected results. No available uncertainty exceeds 0.2, so this dataset has zero valid hidden points. The reviewed paired-component masking remains active, including both operands of every ratio; exclusions are not numerical failures.

All six accuracy records carry `qualified_finite_refinement`; all full/fixed records carry `reproducibility_control`. S2 reviewed isolated and combined magnitude, k, mu, volume, derivative-step and stopping refinements. Full/fixed controls do not claim accuracy refinement or scientific acceptance. Convergence of forest iteration alone does not imply grid convergence; the accuracy qualification comes from S2. No cross-redshift shared-parameter combination is made.

Twelve PNGs in `../.validation/s3/` show both BAO components for all 15 individual spectra, separate joint curves, all three fractional comparisons, and saved weights versus magnitude plus A/P_pixel for each forest. The missing Lyα(LBG) bin-1 panel reflects the authorized exclusion. Inspection covered individual ratios, joint ratios, weights and coefficients: labels, profile/status legends, selection gaps and plotted magnitudes are consistent with saved tables. The plotting command emitted Fontconfig cache warnings but exited successfully and produced all figures. No source or installed-wheel correction was required.

The endpoint forest curves and coefficients are descriptive diagnostics, not a controlled causal decomposition. Weights retain their saved amplitude conventions; curves were not renormalized. The full numerical forest records, recipe identities and qualification evidence are included in `three-profile-tables.json`. Differences between A and P_pixel curves alone do not attribute BAO changes.
