  validation adapters delegate these two variants to that implementation;
  other historical variants and eligibility rules remain unchanged.
- Strict `prepare_forest_weights` adds `early_lyaforecast` and `mcdonald`.
  Both accept direct positive P/B or the existing matching auto-only fiducial
  auxiliary query. Adaptive defaults are rtol=1e-4, minimum three updates,
  three stable transitions, actual doubled-count confirmation and cap 96.
  Explicit counts remain labeled diagnostics. Unconfirmed states raise before
  covariance preparation; the low-level solver retains explicit status/state.
  Legacy, supplied and W12 inverse-variance preparations retain their behavior.
- Revised accuracy uses its own fiducial auto-P3D, P1D, physical response and
  composite magnitude quadrature. It records auxiliary P/B/k/mu, actual
  quadrature, weights, A/P_pixel, stopping controls and residuals per field.
  Per-field preparation is reused and remains fixed through finite differences.
- `profile_definitions.py` identifies revision `early-lyaforecast-2026-09-18`.
  New `profiles.run`/CLI defaults are full-compatibility, fixed-compatibility
  and revised accuracy, bounded to the six-bin DESI-2 15x2pt case. The old
  compatibility alias resolves to full-compatibility in this runner. Historical
  request/schema reconstruction remains available with its original identity;
  explicit historical runner replay uses `recipe_revision=None`.

  forest. Excluded pairs are recorded separately from numerical failures.
- Accuracy trial contract 3 adds the actual rtol=1e-4/1e-5 comparison, retains
  bounded k/mu/magnitude/volume/step and combined refinement, and saves per-trial
  forest states. Contracts 1 and 2 remain historical. Cache matching checks the
