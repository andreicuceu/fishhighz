The six-bin 15×2pt comparison is complete for all five variants at exactly three
updates, and wherever finite nonzero weight convergence was established. The

## Calculation and retained inputs

compatibility_bao.py (local-only path: `../fishhighz/validation/compatibility_bao.py`) and
compare_compatibility_bao.py (local-only path: `../scripts/compare_compatibility_bao.py`) use the
six immutable compatibility records `records-{000,002,004,006,008,010}` under
the Step-12 saved inputs (local-only path: `../.validation/step12-r5-20260914T191855Z/profiles-checked/`).
The original 107 magnitude nodes and signed measure are retained. Mean redshifts
are 2.1175, 2.3525, 2.5875, 2.8225, 3.0575 and 3.2925. The five observed fields
are lya(qso), lya(lbg), QSO, LBG and LAE, giving 15 auto/cross spectra per bin.

All five variants use the same seed and exactly three updates for the fixed-count
comparison. `prefix_intrinsic` is the compatibility control; `sum_intrinsic`
changes the update moments to full-sample sums; `prefix_aliasing` and
`sum_aliasing` add the current moment-based aliasing signal with their respective
moments; `sum_historical` uses the earliest Python aliasing approximation.
Final aliasing and pixel-noise coefficients always use full-sample moments.

Converged coefficients are taken at the saved confirmed count, separately at
rtol=1e-3 and 1e-4. Full-sum states match the stage-3 adaptive stopping count;
prefix states use the stage-2 classification tested against the entire later
saved trajectory through update 96. Prefix convergence here does not extend the
adaptive API's eligibility. Non-auto pair-specific preparations remain recorded
at three updates, explicitly labelled as not entering forest-auto noise. Source
report and stage-2 hashes are cross-checked with stage 3, and each output stores
SHA256 digests of its original report/NPZ and stage-2/3 dependencies.

For each forest auto, the original noise contribution is reconstructed from its
saved coefficients, subtracted from the saved total power, and replaced by the
variant noise. This preserves the intrinsic covariance-redshift signal and
response convention. Galaxy noise, cross powers, observed mean Jacobians,
Fourier modes, volumes, parameter definitions and priors are unchanged. Weights
are fixed in mean derivatives; the saved two-parameter BAO calculation has no
additional priors. All affected Wick covariance entries are rebuilt. Individual
Fisher matrices use each spectrum's own variance; the joint calculation contracts
the full inter-spectrum covariance rather than summing individual matrices.

An individual spectrum requires every forest auto appearing in its variance.
Missing converged autos therefore remove only the affected individual results;
they make the full joint result unavailable, without substituting a partial
joint result or a last attempted state. Zero scratch entries for missing autos
are used only while constructing unaffected variances and never yield a reported
forecast dependent on the missing auto.

## Results

All variants have 90 individual and six joint results at three updates. At
rtol=1e-4 the available results are:

| Variant | Individual / 90 | Joint / 6 | Missing converged forest autos |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| prefix_intrinsic | 36 | 0 | Both forest populations in all bins |
| sum_intrinsic | 80 | 4 | LBG forest, bins 0–1 |
| prefix_aliasing | 85 | 5 | LBG forest, bin 2 |
| sum_aliasing | 80 | 4 | LBG forest, bins 0–1 |
| sum_historical | 90 | 6 | None |

The 36 available prefix-intrinsic entries are galaxy-only spectra. Their inclusion
in the converged table is an absence of forest-weight dependence, not evidence
of prefix-intrinsic weight convergence. Galaxy-only individual Fisher matrices
and uncertainties are bitwise unchanged for every variant and mode.

The largest individual converged/three-update change is +37.681% in transverse
LBG-forest auto uncertainty for prefix aliasing in bin 0. That point is hidden
by the uncertainty cut and remains in the saved tables. Corresponding largest
increases for sum intrinsic, sum aliasing and historical sum are +6.471%, +6.318%
and +4.539%, respectively, in transverse LBG-forest auto uncertainty in bin 2.
Available joint converged/three-update changes are smaller: approximately
−0.0371% to +0.945% across components, variants and bins.

Tightening the tolerance was checked for 390 available spectrum/bin/variant
results (780 uncertainty components), including five results hidden by the plot
cut. The maximum change is 8.669219007106932e-7 fractionally, or 0.0000866922%.
This satisfies the assigned <0.1% criterion without changing the stopping rule.
Unavailable cases remain unavailable and do not contribute to this comparison.

Baseline individual uncertainties reproduce exactly. The maximum saved relative
joint Fisher discrepancy is 4.67e-17 and the maximum joint-error discrepancy is
9.11e-17. Independent scalar Wick covariance construction at Fourier cells 17,
101 and 777 agrees exactly; independent per-cell solves agree with the vectorized
Fisher contraction to at most 1.89e-15. These checks are repeated in each bin for
the three-update sum-aliasing case.
